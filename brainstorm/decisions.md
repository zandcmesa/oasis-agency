# Oasis Agency — Decisions

## Brand & Naming
- Decision: "Oasis" is the working name and likely final name
- Why: Faith-rooted, resonant, owner has affinity for it — fits the positioning of being a refuge from janky templated agency work
- Open: Not finalized — owner open to other faith-rooted alternatives before committing

## Target Market
- Decision: Churches and ministries of all sizes
- Why: Owner isn't narrowing yet — the value prop works across size ranges
- Note: First real prospect is Cornerstone Church (brother-in-law is senior pastor) — mid-size, multiple campuses implied

## Positioning
- Decision: "The agency that can actually build things"
- Why: Incumbent agency (Rose and Gold) can't solve technical problems (sermon search, Planning Center integration) — that's the gap
- Differentiator: Faith-aligned + bold design + real engineering depth (not just template management)

## Lead Offer
- Decision: Full-service retainer as the primary model
- Why: Recurring revenue > project-based; churches are loyal clients; most have fragmented point solutions that need a trusted fixer
- Pricing structure: TBD — to be designed from scratch (tiered vs. base + usage add-ons is open)

## First Services to Build Out (Priority Order)
1. Church website design & build (Next.js, custom)
2. Planning Center / Church Center seamless integration (eliminate the "two website" problem)
3. Sermon search & library (YouTube auto-transcription + search UI)
4. Email & text campaigns (strategy + execution)
5. Brand & visual identity (logo, color, voice)
6. Promotional video (b-roll production — owner has proven this already)

## First Prospect: Cornerstone Church
- Pain points: sermon search/filter, two-website experience (main site + Planning Center), no email/text campaign support
- Status: Pastor is open, needs to pitch church council — needs something real to show
- Constraint: Must assure existing systems won't break during transition
- Strategy: Build a pitch/demo around their specific pain points before asking for the business
- Pricing: Free — structured as a **Founding Partnership**, not a favor
- What Cornerstone provides in return: honest feedback, case study permission, testimonial
- What they get if they continue: founding partner pricing (best rate Oasis will ever offer)
- Requires: a simple one-page written agreement even at zero cost, to keep the relationship professional

## Tech Stack (Client Work)
- Decision: Primarily custom/headless Next.js for complex features; Planning Center API integrations; flexible per client
- Why: Engineering depth is the differentiator — not being locked to one platform is part of the value

## Agency Website
- Decision: Built in Next.js (custom code — the site IS the portfolio)
- Tone: Bold + modern design chops + warm + faith-aligned
- Goal: Credibility first at launch, then progressive lead gen (SEO, discovery call booking)

## Staffing
- Decision: Solo nights/weekends initially
- Why: Validate before committing — keep Google job until revenue justifies the leap
- When to hire: When solo capacity is the bottleneck, not demand

## Connection to Generational Wealth Goal
- This agency is the primary vehicle for building a business the owner's children could inherit
- Revenue target: Eventually multiple millions/year
- Timeline: Start nights/weekends, validate, then consider the leap
