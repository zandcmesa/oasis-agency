# Imagery for the services rail

Two cards have no photography: AI agent workers & teams (02) and Digital presence strategy (03). Both currently use the typographic green panel. This file holds the art direction and prompts so they can be generated in one sitting.

## Shared rules

- Palette: paper `#F6F1EA`, ink `#0E141B`, oasis green `#1A5C4A`, soft clay `#C4A484`, spring `#8FCDB0`. Backgrounds should be paper or green so the images sit inside the card without a visible edge.
- Aspect: 4:3 for mobile, roughly 1:1.2 for the desktop half-panel. Generate square at 2048 and crop.
- Lighting: soft, single key light from upper left, no harsh shadows. Editorial, not stock.
- No text baked into the image.

## 02 — Agent avatars

One character per named worker. Same family, different personalities. Think Pixar-adjacent plush toys photographed in a studio, not clip art.

Base prompt:

> A cute 3D character, small fluffy creature with soft fur, round body, big expressive eyes, sitting upright, studio product photography on a plain background colored #F6F1EA, soft diffused lighting from upper left, shallow depth of field, rendered in the style of a high-end animation studio, clean, no text, centered, square composition.

Per-worker variations:

- **After-Hours Catcher** — fur in oasis green `#1A5C4A` with a spring `#8FCDB0` belly, wearing a tiny headset, one ear perked, holding a glowing phone, calm and alert. Nighttime mood: background shifts to ink `#0E141B` with a soft moon-colored rim light.
- **Front Desk SMS** — fur in soft clay `#C4A484` with a paper-colored face, a small speech bubble shaped tuft on its head, holding a tiny tablet, friendly and quick, slight lean forward like it's about to answer.
- **Beauty pack (both together)** — the two characters side by side on a small rounded pedestal, same lighting, looking at each other.

Future workers get the same base prompt with a new fur color and one prop that names the job.

## 03 — Digital presence strategy

The audit finds the gaps between a brand's touchpoints and makes them read as one thing. The image should show fragmentation resolving into coherence, shot like a magazine still life.

Three directions, in order of recommendation:

1. **The contact sheet.** A grid of small printed squares laid on paper: a business card, a phone screen, a storefront sign, a social post, an email header, a van door. In the top-left they are mismatched colors and fonts. Moving to the bottom-right they progressively become the same green and the same mark. Overhead shot, hard paper background, soft shadows. Reads as "we make all of this match."

   > Overhead editorial still life on a plain #F6F1EA paper surface: a 4x4 grid of small physical brand touchpoints (business card, phone showing a social post, tiny storefront sign, envelope, van door decal, tote bag). Top-left items are mismatched in color and typography; toward the bottom-right they become a single consistent identity in deep green #1A5C4A with one simple circular mark. Soft diffused daylight, gentle shadows, shallow depth of field, shot on medium format, no legible text, muted and refined.

2. **The signal.** One green circular mark repeated at different scales across an abstract cityscape of paper cutouts: a billboard, a shop window, a phone, a screen in a window. Everything else is desaturated paper; the mark is the only saturated color. Reads as "instantly identifiable."

   > Layered paper-cutout diorama of a small street scene in monochrome cream #F6F1EA and clay #C4A484 tones. A single deep green #1A5C4A circular mark appears repeatedly on a billboard, a shop awning, a smartphone held by a paper figure, and a window poster. Soft studio lighting, tilt-shift depth of field, editorial product photography, no legible text.

3. **The lens.** A magnifying glass or a sheet of green-tinted glass laid over a messy collage of brand fragments. Inside the glass, the fragments align into a clean grid. Literal "audit" metaphor; strongest for a page header, weakest for a card.

   > Editorial overhead photograph: a scattered collage of mismatched paper brand fragments on cream paper; a rectangular pane of translucent green glass #1A5C4A lies across the middle, and everything seen through the glass is neatly aligned in a grid and unified in one color. Soft natural light, subtle refraction, shallow depth of field, no legible text.

Recommendation: direction 1 for the card. It shows the outcome rather than the process, and it still reads at card size.
